# CVC 2026 Schedule Verification

The public **Schedule & Results** route was visually checked after the 2026 workbook-imported matchups were grouped by `week_number`.

The route displays chronological sections from **Week 1** through **Week 14**, with each section containing that week’s five imported matchups. The schedule board retains the existing CVC result-state display and continues to render the imported franchise pairings from the live league query.
