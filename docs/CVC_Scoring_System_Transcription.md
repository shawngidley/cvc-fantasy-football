# CVC Scoring System Transcription

## Source and validation status

This transcription was verified from the supplied CVC scoring-system image in ordered, overlapping crops. The scoring lines and D/ST attribution language were reconciled across the overlap boundaries.

## Verified scoring rules

| Unit | Scoring rule |
| --- | --- |
| QB | 1 point per 20 passing yards; 1 point per 10 rushing or receiving yards; 4 points per passing touchdown; 6 points per rushing or receiving touchdown; minus 1 point per interception. |
| RB / WR / TE | 1 point per 20 passing yards; 1 point per 10 rushing or receiving yards; 4 points per passing touchdown; 6 points per rushing or receiving touchdown; 0.5 points per reception. |
| PK | 1 point per extra point; 0.1 point per yard for field goals. |
| D/ST | 2 points per fumble recovery; 2 points per interception; 2 points per sack; 6 points per touchdown; 5 points per safety. |
| D/ST points allowed | 15 points for 0 points allowed; 10 for 1–6; 5 for 7–13; 3 for 14–20. |

## Source notes requiring interpretation

The source includes a named historical note involving Dante Hall and the Kansas City defense sharing a punt-return touchdown. It also states that a defensive touchdown or fumble-recovery score is valid only when the team defense started the play on the field. The final CVC scoring engine will treat this as a D/ST attribution rule rather than incorrectly awarding a D/ST defensive touchdown or recovery to an offensive player.

The source also says that games are not final until Friday morning, after NFL stat corrections have been finalized. Automatic Tank01 finalization must therefore retain a correction window instead of locking a matchup immediately after the final whistle.
